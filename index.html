About me

With over three years of experience in the data field, I work with modeling, processing, and analyzing information to support strategic decision-making. I have experience in creating interactive dashboards, monitoring project indicators, and developing impactful business presentations. Additionally, I work with data governance, ensuring data quality, compliance, and alignment with organizational needs, always respecting data protection laws. My approach combines technical precision and analytical vision to transform data into valuable insights.

I have experience working at Banco Safra, Afya, and the Court of Justice of the State of Bahia. At Afya, there was no monitoring process in place, and after I joined, we implemented over three monitoring reports. These included an assessment of data maturity, a report tracking access activities on our governance platform, and a report ensuring that dashboards created within the organization complied with established policies.
